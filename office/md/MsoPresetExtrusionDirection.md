# MsoPresetExtrusionDirection

**Type:** Enumeration  
**Library:** Microsoft Office 16.0 Object Library  

Specifies the direction that the extrusion's sweep path takes away from the extruded shape (the front face of the extrusion). Used with the PresetExtrusionDirection property of the ThreeDFormat object.

## Constants (10)

- `msoPresetExtrusionDirectionMixed` = -2  
  Return value only; indicates a combination of the other states.
- `msoExtrusionBottomRight` = 1  
  Lower right
- `msoExtrusionBottom` = 2  
  Lower part
- `msoExtrusionBottomLeft` = 3  
  Lower left
- `msoExtrusionRight` = 4  
  Right
- `msoExtrusionNone` = 5  
  No extrusion
- `msoExtrusionLeft` = 6  
  Left
- `msoExtrusionTopRight` = 7  
  Upper right
- `msoExtrusionTop` = 8  
  Upper part
- `msoExtrusionTopLeft` = 9  
  Upper left
