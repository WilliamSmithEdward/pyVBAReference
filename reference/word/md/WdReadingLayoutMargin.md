# WdReadingLayoutMargin

**Type:** Enumeration  
**Library:** Microsoft Word 16.0 Object Library  

Specifies whether margins are visible or hidden when a document is viewed in Full Screen Reading view.

## Constants (3)

- `wdAutomaticMargin` = 0  
  Shows the pages without margins.
- `wdSuppressMargin` = 1  
  Microsoft Word determines automatically whether to show or hide the margins based on the available space.
- `wdFullMargin` = 2  
  Shows the pages with margins.
