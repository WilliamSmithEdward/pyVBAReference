# MsoPresetLightingDirection

**Type:** Enumeration  
**Library:** Microsoft Office 16.0 Object Library  

Specifies the location of lighting on an extruded (three-dimensional) shape relative to the shape.

## Constants (10)

- `msoPresetLightingDirectionMixed` = -2  
  Not supported.
- `msoLightingTopLeft` = 1  
  Lighting comes from the upper-left.
- `msoLightingTop` = 2  
  Lighting comes from the upper part.
- `msoLightingTopRight` = 3  
  Lighting comes from the upper-right.
- `msoLightingLeft` = 4  
  Lighting comes from the left.
- `msoLightingNone` = 5  
  No lighting.
- `msoLightingRight` = 6  
  Lighting comes from the right.
- `msoLightingBottomLeft` = 7  
  Lighting comes from the lower-left.
- `msoLightingBottom` = 8  
  Lighting comes from the lower part.
- `msoLightingBottomRight` = 9  
  Lighting comes from the lower-right.
