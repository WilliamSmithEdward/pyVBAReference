# MsoTextureAlignment

**Type:** Enumeration  
**Library:** Microsoft Office 16.0 Object Library  

Specifies the alignment (the origin of the coordinate grid) for the tiling of the texture fill.

## Constants (10)

- `msoTextureAlignmentMixed` = -2  
  Return value only; indicates a combination of the other states.
- `msoTextureTopLeft` = 0  
  Top-left alignment
- `msoTextureTop` = 1  
  Top alignment
- `msoTextureTopRight` = 2  
  Top-right alignment
- `msoTextureLeft` = 3  
  Left alignment
- `msoTextureCenter` = 4  
  Center alignment
- `msoTextureRight` = 5  
  Right alignment
- `msoTextureBottomLeft` = 6  
  Bottom-left alignment
- `msoTextureBottom` = 7  
  Bottom alignment
- `msoTextureBottomRight` = 8  
  Bottom-right alignment
